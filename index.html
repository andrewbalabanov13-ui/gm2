Solar System Stars Black Holes Facts

🌎 Our Solar System

Our solar system is made up of the Sun and everything that orbits it. This includes eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other objects.

☀️

The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of our solar system. Its gravity keeps the planets in orbit.

🌍

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world currently known to support life.

🔴

Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because iron minerals in its soil give it a reddish appearance.

🪐

Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant famous for its spectacular system of icy rings.

⭐ Stars

Stars are enormous balls of extremely hot gas. They produce energy through nuclear fusion in their cores.

🌟 How Stars Shine

Stars generate energy through nuclear fusion. In many stars, hydrogen atoms combine to form helium.

💥 Supernovas

Some massive stars end their lives in enormous explosions called supernovas.

🌌 Galaxies

Galaxies are enormous collections of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.

🕳️ Black Holes

A black hole is an object with gravity so strong that nothing that crosses its event horizon can escape, including light.

Black holes can form when massive stars collapse at the end of their lives. Supermassive black holes can also exist at the centers of galaxies.

The boundary around a black hole beyond which escape is impossible is called the event horizon.
